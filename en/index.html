Features

On-device translation

The AI translator runs on your iPhone or Mac. Chapter text is never sent to a third party.

Hán Việt register

Keeps the names, ranks and terms readers of xianxia expect. Machine translation can be inaccurate.

You decide

Chapters are fetched and translated only when you open or choose to download them.

Comfortable reader

Light and dark themes, system text sizes, bookmarks and remembered position.

Your names

With Pro on iPhone, edit how proper names are translated for each story.

No account

Library, progress and bookmarks stay on your device; sync uses your own iCloud.

Private by design

Altos runs no server for user data and cannot read your iCloud data. The free iPhone tier shows Google AdMob ads; what ads may collect is described in the Privacy Policy.

Two apps

Altos for iPhone

For iPhone with iOS 27. On-device translation needs an iPhone with 8 GB of memory; the translator is about 2.6 GB, Wi‑Fi only, with your consent.

Altos for Mac

A separate Mac App Store app for Mac with Apple silicon. No ads.

FAQ

Does Altos provide stories?

No. Altos is a reading and translation tool. We provide only a descriptive index; we do not host or distribute chapter text. Copyright belongs to the authors and rights holders.

Is story text sent to a server?

No. Translation runs on your own iPhone or Mac.

Is the translation accurate?

It is generated automatically and may be wrong or incomplete. It is not a licensed translation and is for your private reading.

Do I need an account?

No. Paid plans are managed by your Apple Account.

Who is it for?

Adults 18+, as stories may contain violence or mature themes.

I own the copyright. Where do I complain?

See the Copyright page: we respond within 5 working days.
