Common questions

Manage or cancel a plan

iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Mac: App Store › account › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Restore purchases

Open Settings in the app and restore purchases.

Refunds

Refunds follow Apple’s policy and are requested from Apple.

Delete data

Delete per story in Settings › Storage, or delete all data in the app.

Device gets warm

When warm, the app translates only the open chapter; when hot, it pauses until it cools.
