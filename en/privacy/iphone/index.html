Altos Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 — effective (pending)

  1. Controller. (pending), (pending); data-protection contact (pending).
  2. Legal basis. Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP.
  3. What we do not collect. We run no servers for user data, require no account and cannot read your iCloud data.
  4. On your device. Library, reading progress and position, chapters read, reading history, bookmarks, settings, consent choices, downloaded chapters, translations and the app's processing log, kept until you delete them.
  5. iCloud. If you enable iCloud (or computer) backup, your device backup includes the data in section 4, downloaded chapters and translations included; Apple manages it for you and we cannot access it. On the sync-enabled build, library, progress and bookmarks sync through your own private CloudKit database; story content is not synced.
  1. Advertising. On the free tier the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and UMP SDKs may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your tracking permission), IP address and coarse location derived from it, app and ad interactions, crash, performance and other diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud; data is transferred abroad and handled under Google's policy.
  1. Tracking. Asked only if you choose personalised ads; declining still shows non-personalised ads.
  1. Payments. Handled by Apple; we never receive card details.
  2. Diagnostics. Altos's own code collects and sends no diagnostics; Google's ad SDK may collect diagnostic data as in section 6 (free tier). Crash reports reach us only if you turn on sharing with app developers in iOS Settings.
  3. Third-party websites. The app downloads the Index and page templates from our public repository on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), without any of your data. When you open or choose to download chapters, your device contacts the novel's source websites (the current list is in Settings › Legal › Source websites, updated with the Index), which may set cookies under their own policies; each website has its own cookie store in the app. Cover images load from the source websites while you browse novels, so they may receive your IP address before you open any chapter.
  1. On-device AI. Translation runs on your iPhone; story text is never sent to a third-party AI service.
  1. Consent. Accepting the Terms is a service agreement, not consent to data processing. That you read this Policy is recorded on its own (date and version), apart from the Terms. We ask separately, and optionally, for personalised ads (with iOS tracking permission and, where the law requires it, Google's consent form). Withdraw at any time with the "Quảng cáo cá nhân hoá" switch in Settings › Quyền riêng tư, or iOS Settings › Privacy › Tracking; withdrawal does not affect earlier processing.
  2. Retention and deletion. Kept until you delete it: Settings › Storage deletes a novel's chapters and translations; "Xoá toàn bộ dữ liệu" deletes on-device data and the app's iCloud zone. Older device backups follow your Apple backup settings.
  3. Your rights. To know, access, correct, delete, restrict, object and complain: (pending).
  4. Security. iOS data protection and Apple's iCloud security.
  5. Children. Not intended for anyone under 18.
  6. Third parties. Google and Apple process data under their own policies (links in sections 6 and 8).
  7. Changes. Material changes require your renewed consent.

The Vietnamese version prevails.
