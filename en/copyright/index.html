Copyright and takedown

Altos respects copyright. We do not host or distribute chapter text; the index holds descriptions only. If you own the rights and believe an entry infringes them, send a notice with:

A notice needs

  • Your details and your standing as owner or authorised agent
  • The protected work
  • The index entry or source website concerned
  • A statement that the notice is accurate

How we handle it

We respond within 5 working days: remove the entry, add the work or website to the blocklist the app applies, and delete it from devices when the app updates. Repeat-infringing sources are blocked entirely, under Article 198b of Vietnam’s IP Law.
